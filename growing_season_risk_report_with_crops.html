Historical baseline comparison

This first version summarizes the selected period. It does not yet compare that period with a multi-year climate baseline or predict future weather.

NASA POWER climate data represents estimates for a geographic coordinate, not direct field sensor readings. Soil wetness values are modeled using MERRA-2 and are not SMAP satellite measurements. USDA soil information represents soil survey estimates for a map unit, not measurements of a specific field. Rainfall is not equivalent to irrigation or total water available to crops. Crop reference values are draft and unverified; temperature thresholds are user-selected screening values, not universal crop-damage thresholds. Historical conditions do not predict future weather, crop yields, or planting success. Farmers should consult local agricultural extension resources and field measurements when making decisions.
